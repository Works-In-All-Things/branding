# Works In All Things Brand Guidelines

## Source of truth

Start with `brand-kit.json`. It provides structured paths, colors, type, logo lockups, proposed-field status, and agent-ready rules. This guide explains the human decisions behind it. All paths are relative to the repository root.

## Brand character

Works In All Things should feel thoughtful, capable, plain-spoken, humble, and service-oriented. The work supports church staff and volunteers using Rock RMS, so lead with a practical outcome and make the next step easy to understand.

Write in sentence case. Prefer specific verbs, honest constraints, and useful technical detail. Avoid inflated claims such as “revolutionary,” “ultimate,” “seamless,” “game-changing,” and “transformation.”

Good: “Clear setup guides, a FAQ, and a real person to email.”

Avoid: “The ultimate platform for church transformation.”

## Color and type

Use Signal Orange (`#E87121`) for action and emphasis, never for long passages of text. The source-backed dark foundation is Ink (`#0C0D10`) and Raised (`#14161B`), with Brand Grey (`#E5E5E5`) and Muted (`#A3A9B6`) for light text.

Use Lato at 400 or 700. Keep headings short, useful, and in sentence case. The interface and semantic mappings in the JSON are proposed implementation tokens, not approved brand standards.

## Logo usage

Use the supplied primary raster wordmark intact where the name needs to be read. Use the W site mark only for favicons or compact, already-established contexts. Do not redraw, crop, stretch, recolor, or put a logo on busy imagery.

Clear space and minimum-size rules are proposed interim guidance, not confirmed policy. The derived light/dark SVG mark variants are also proposed; the published full wordmark remains raster-only.

## Open decisions

Before publishing a new permanent asset or making a public claim, consult `proposed_fields` in `brand-kit.json`. The owner still needs to confirm: tagline, locations, clear space/minimum size, derived SVG variants, UI semantic tokens, and imagery direction.
